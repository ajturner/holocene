Conversion: HE = CE year + 10,000. For a BCE year, HE = 10,001 − BCE (there is no year zero). Dates marked ~ are approximate; before about 8000 HE they carry uncertainties of a century or more. Writing appears near 6700 HE, so everything ever written down fits in the last 44% of row 1.
