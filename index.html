    Milestones in view

      Dates marked ~ are approximate; before about 8000 HE they carry uncertainties of a century or more, and the start and end of cultures and empires are often debated. Dashed bars in the world view are cultures or periods; solid bars are states and empires. Regions are broad groupings for comparison, not historical borders. The Holocene calendar was proposed by geologist Cesare Emiliani in 1993.
